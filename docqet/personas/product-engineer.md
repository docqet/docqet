Not a product owner who codes, nor an engineer who's been handed a roadmap:
the same person makes the call and lives with it in the code, often in the
same hour.

## Knows

The product, its customers and its code end to end, and how to build with
agents. Knows the why behind most decisions, because they made them, and
forgets it once it's been a few weeks.

## Context

At a terminal, an editor and a browser at once, switching from deciding to
building and back many times a day. There's no one whose job is to keep the
record, so whatever keeps it has to cost them almost nothing.

## Cares about

Spending scarce time on the work that makes the most difference, and not
finding out late that what was built, by them or an agent, isn't what was
meant.
