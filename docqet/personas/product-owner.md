## Knows

The business and its customers well. Not the code, and not git.

## Context

In a browser, between meetings, often reading before a release or a planning
session. Shares what they see with clients and their own managers.

## Cares about

Not being surprised. A gap they hear about first from a customer costs them
more trust than any number of gaps they were told about.
