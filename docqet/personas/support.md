## Knows

The customers, what they're trying to get done, and where they get stuck.
Not the code, and not git.

## Context

In a browser, often in the middle of a customer conversation. Needs an
answer in a minute, not a meeting.

## Cares about

Not promising a customer something the software doesn't do, or calling a
deliberate choice a bug that's about to be fixed.
