## Knows

Their own field, and almost nothing about this product: not its words, not
the problems it's there to solve, and not how it's built.

## Context

In their first days or weeks, reading more than doing. Every question they
ask takes someone else's time, so they'd rather find the answer themselves.

## Cares about

Building an accurate picture fast, without having to know what to ask first.
