## Needs

- To get each day's takings into the accounting software without typing them in.
- To invoice every company once a month, and see what each one owes.
- To keep companies within the credit the hotel allows them.

## Knows

Bookkeeping, and the hotel's accounting software, well. Less about how the front
desk works.

## Context

Part time, two or three days a week, at a desk in the back office. Month end is
their busiest time.

## Expects

Days that are closed and stay closed, invoice numbers that run in order, and an
export their accounting software takes as it is.

## Cares about

Books that balance. A day that changes after they've exported it means finding
the difference by hand.
