id: billed-stay
name: Billed stay
description: >-
  What a guest is charged for a stay: its nights, at the rate agreed when it
  was booked.
confused-with:
  - term: Booking
    summary: >-
      a guest's hold on a room, which the booking app keeps. Billing doesn't
      keep rooms free or taken, only what each stay costs.
---

A billed stay puts one room charge per night on the guest's [bill](bill.md),
at its agreed rate, whatever the rate is on the night.
