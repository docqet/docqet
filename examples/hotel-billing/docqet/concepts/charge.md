id: charge
name: Charge
description: >-
  One amount a guest owes, such as a night's room, a breakfast or a night's
  city tax.
---

Each charge is on one [bill](bill.md), and on the
[business day](business-day.md) it was added. A correction is a charge for the
opposite amount, pointing at the charge it corrects.
