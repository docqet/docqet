## Needs

- To know the day was closed and every guest charged for the night, without
  staying up to do it by hand.
- To close the day themselves when the audit didn't run on its own.

## Knows

The night audit's steps, from years of doing them on paper. Less at ease with
software that does them for them.

## Context

At the front desk overnight, alone, with few guests around and no one to ask.

## Expects

To see, at a glance, that tonight's audit has run, and what it charged.

## Cares about

Not handing over to the morning shift with yesterday still open.
