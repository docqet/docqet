## Needs

- To keep the company's payment methods current, and choose the one charged by
  default.
- To find every invoice, with its tax and payment status, and download it to
  file.
- To be told at once when a payment fails, and pay the unpaid invoice, so the
  blocks it bought aren't left waiting.

## Knows

Accounts payable and the company's cards. Little about the engineering: a block
is a line item.

## Context

Laptop, a few times a month in batches, or when a failed-payment notice arrives.
Pressed at month end.

## Expects

An invoice list, statuses and a downloadable PDF, like any vendor portal.

## Cares about

Amounts that match and are easy to file. Gives up if invoices are hard to find
or a failed payment is unclear.
