A partnership has one manager, a person, who can manage several. It has the name
its manager gives it, a referral code, the clients attributed to it, and terms:
its share, what it's a share of, and how long it earns on each client, or no time
limit.

Its share is a [payout share](payout-share.md) of the
[capacity payout basis](capacity-payout-basis.md) of the engineering capacity its
attributed clients purchase and have fulfilled. How long it earns on each client
is its earning window.

Whether it pays a person or a business is settled when its payout account is set
up. Its terms are set out in its Partner [Agreement](agreement.md).
