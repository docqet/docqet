name: Conversation
description: >-
  A conversation is the talk about one subject on a [capacity
  block](capacity-block.md), in its organization's [space](space.md). A block
  has any number, each on a subject of its own.
---

Someone starts a conversation by giving it a subject, such as "Rate file
format". With the messaging provider it opens with the block's number before
the subject, "BLK-0037 - Rate file format", since the space holds every
block's conversations. Everyone in the space can read and join it; it has no
people of its own.

What's said stays with the messaging provider. The app reads only how many
messages a conversation has, when the latest was sent, and how many one of
Exalynt's people hasn't read, which the messaging provider can't say for a
client's.

## Lifecycle

1. Started

A conversation is recorded once the messaging provider has started it, and stays
for good.
