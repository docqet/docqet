A team has a name, a set of members, at most one of them its lead, and the
[organizations](../../accounts/concepts/organization.md) it's assigned to as clients. A team can support
any number of clients, and a client can have any number of teams. A person is on
at most one team.

A team lead is an engineer an admin designates as a team's lead, with everything
an engineer can do too.
