domain: partnerships
name: Invitations are limited
respects: [constraint:no-unwanted-bulk-email]
---

A partnership can send at most 50 new invitations in a day, and revoking one
doesn't free up another. Each invitation can be resent at most once a day. So
nobody is flooded with email from Exalynt.
