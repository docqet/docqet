name: A partnership's payout account is its own
why: >-
  Partnerships are separate payees, whether a person or a business stands
  behind them, and keep their account when they change managers.
---

A partnership has its own payout account, apart from its manager's and from any
other partnership's, so someone managing several sets each one up.
