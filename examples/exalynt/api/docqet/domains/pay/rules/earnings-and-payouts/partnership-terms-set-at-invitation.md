name: Partnership terms set at invitation
respects: [constraint:agreements-set-pay]
---

A partnership earns on the terms Exalynt set when inviting it: its share, its
basis, and its earning window. The default is 5% of the engineering capacity
referred clients purchase and have fulfilled within 12 months.
