## Clients

A business signs up on its own and gets to its first block booked into a week:

1. Someone at the business signs up, without an invitation, and creates an
   organization.
2. One of its Admins accepts the
   [Client Agreement](../../agreements/capabilities/client-agreements.md) on its
   behalf, before anyone can use the portal for it.
3. Exalynt gives the organization a team as it signs up, when one has an
   engineer offering time. If none does, it waits without a team until an admin
   assigns one, and can still buy.
4. The client sees what's still needed before work can start, and adds a
   payment method and company details.
5. They [buy a block](../../capacity/capabilities/capacity-purchasing.md), and
   [schedule it](../../capacity/capabilities/scheduling.md) for a preferred
   week.

A client who signed up with a partner's referral code, link or invitation is
attributed to that partnership, and told when signing up that the partner may
earn from it. Others join later by an Admin's invitation, each with a role.

## Engineers

An admin invites the engineer by email, choosing their team, if any, and
setting the Commercial Terms of their
[Engineer Agreement](../../agreements/capabilities/engineer-agreements.md). The
engineer accepts, signing up first if they need to, reads and accepts the
agreement, and then sets their availability, the bank account their payouts go
to, and a short bio, until they're ready for client work. Someone who already
has an account keeps it, and gains the console alongside what they had.
