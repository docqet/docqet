name: Invitations are limited
---

An organization can send at most 50 invitations in a day, and so can each
person, across every organization they invite people to. Revoking one, or its
being accepted, doesn't free up another. So nobody is flooded with email from
Exalynt, even by someone who signs up just to send it.
