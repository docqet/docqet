# Security

Docket is a **Prototype**, and only its latest commit on `main` is
supported.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please don't open a public issue. Report it privately through GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/exalynt/docket/security/advisories/new),
with what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it affects.

We'll acknowledge it, keep you updated, and credit you when it's fixed,
unless you'd rather we didn't.

## Known limits

The Docket server has no sign-in yet. Until it does, a server holding real
projects must only be reachable by the people allowed to see them, such as
on a private network. That's a known gap rather than a vulnerability.
