# Sam, front desk clerk

## Who

Works the front desk of a small hotel, taking bookings by phone and in person,
and handling changes when guests' plans move.

## Knows

The hotel's rooms and rates by heart. Comfortable with booking software, but
not technical.

## Context

At a desktop at the front desk, often with a guest waiting or on the phone.
Interrupted constantly.

## Expects

Talks about stays, nights and rooms, not reservations or inventory. Used to a
paper diary that shows which rooms are taken on which nights.

## Cares about

Never putting two guests in one room. Gives up on anything that keeps a waiting
guest waiting longer.

## Based on

Invented for this example.
