# Ana, housekeeper

Cleans and readies the rooms between guests.

## Needs

- To know which rooms to clean first, so arriving guests don't wait for a
  room.
- To tell the front desk a room is ready without walking back down to say so.

## Knows

The building, floor by floor, and how long each kind of room takes. Uses a
phone every day, but rarely for work.

## Context

On their feet, moving from room to room with a trolley, a phone in their pocket.
Gloves on much of the time, so anything they do has to take a tap or two.

## Expects

A list of rooms, in the order they'll do them. Talks about rooms being clean,
dirty or ready.

## Cares about

Not being rushed by the front desk for a room they didn't know was urgent.
