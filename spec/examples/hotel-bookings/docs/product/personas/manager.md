# David, hotel manager

Runs the hotel, and answers for its takings to the owners.

## Needs

- To set what each room costs, night by night, so busy dates earn more.

## Knows

The local market and the hotel's busy seasons. Reads a spreadsheet well, and
expects software to do the arithmetic for them.

## Context

At a desk in the back office, a few times a week, in between everything else
running a hotel brings.

## Expects

A calendar of rates, like the one they keep in a spreadsheet today.

## Cares about

Rates they can change in a minute when a big event is announced, and being sure
the front desk is quoting them.
