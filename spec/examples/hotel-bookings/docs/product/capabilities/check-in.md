# Check-in

Clerks check guests in when they arrive and hand over their room.

## What people can do

### Front desk clerk

- See today's arrivals.
- Check a guest in, and give them their room.
- Move a guest to another room that's free for the rest of their stay.

## Success

- A guest with a booking is checked in within two minutes of reaching the desk.
