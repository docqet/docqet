# Waitlist

Clerks put a guest on a waitlist when every room is taken, and offer them the
first room that frees up for their stay.

## Covers

- Clerks see who is waiting for each night.

## Open decisions

- **Who goes first:** whoever asked first, or whoever's stay fits the free
  nights best?
