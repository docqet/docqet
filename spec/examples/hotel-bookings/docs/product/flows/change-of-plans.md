# Change of plans

A guest whose dates change either moves their booking or cancels it, and ends up
with a stay that fits or a refund.

- **People:** front desk clerk, guest
- **Starts when:** a guest asks to change the dates of their booking.
- **Done when:** the guest has a booking for their new dates, or their booking
  is cancelled and they know what they'll be refunded.

## Steps

1. **Guest** asks the front desk to change their dates.
2. **Front desk clerk** checks whether the guest's room is free for the new
   dates.
3. **Front desk clerk** moves the booking to the new dates.
4. **Guest** gets confirmation of the new dates.

## Variations

- **The room isn't free:** the clerk offers another free room. If none suits,
  the clerk tells the guest what they'd be refunded and cancels the booking.
