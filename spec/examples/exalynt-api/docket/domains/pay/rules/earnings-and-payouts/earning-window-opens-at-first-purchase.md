---
docket: rule
id: earning-window-opens-at-first-purchase
domain: pay
name: Earning window opens at first purchase
---

A client's earning window opens at the first block it buys while attributed to
the partnership, and lasts as many months as the partnership's Earning Window
says, or has no end when its terms set no time limit. Blocks it bought before
it was attributed don't open it, and earn the partnership nothing.
