## Enrolling a partner

1. An admin invites the partner by email and sets the partnership's terms. The
   invitation creates a new partnership for them to manage.
2. The partner signs up, if they need to, and reads the
   [Partner Agreement](../../agreements/capabilities/partner-agreements.md) the
   invitation offers.
3. They name the partnership and accept, in one step: that accepts the
   invitation and the Partner Agreement together.
4. They see the partnership's terms, and its referral code and sign-up link to
   share, or invite people by email.
5. They see what's left before they can be paid, and set up the
   [payout account](../../pay/capabilities/partner-payout-accounts.md) the
   partnership's earnings go to.

Someone who already has an account keeps it. A partner who manages several
partnerships switches between them, viewing one at a time.
