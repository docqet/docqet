---
docket: rule
id: a-focus-is-its-own-record
domain: capacity
name: A focus is its own record
---

A focus is its own record, and can be on several of its client's blocks. A
block has at most one focus: giving it a new focus, or one of its client's
existing focuses, takes the place of the one it had, which remains. A focus
needs a title, editing it keeps it the same focus and changes it on every block
it's on, and taking it off a block keeps the focus. A block can't be given
another client's focus.
