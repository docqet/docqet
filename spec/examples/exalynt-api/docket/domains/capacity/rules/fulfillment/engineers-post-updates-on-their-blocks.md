docket: rule
id: engineers-post-updates-on-their-blocks
domain: capacity
name: Engineers post updates on their blocks
---

Whoever can manage a block posts updates on it: its engineer, their lead, or an
admin, and nobody else. An update is about its block, and about the block's
focus at the time when it has one. A block with no focus still takes updates.
An update's attachments are its client's objects, never another organization's.
