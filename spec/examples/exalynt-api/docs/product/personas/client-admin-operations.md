## Needs

- To buy blocks and schedule them for the weeks that suit the business, in a few
  minutes between meetings.
- To see where the work stands each week, and the named engineer on each block,
  without having to ask.
- To know what the work will cost and how many weeks it takes, from Exalynt's
  estimates, so they can plan the budget.
- To bring colleagues in and give each the right role, so others can follow the
  work or pay the invoices.

## Knows

Their business and its processes in depth. Doesn't code. Has worked with
software vendors, but blocks of capacity are new.

## Context

Laptop, between meetings, once or twice a week around checkpoints. Buys and
schedules in short, interrupted sittings.

## Expects

To talk in weeks, budget and outcomes, never hours. "Sprint" and "ticket" mean
little. Expects plain language and ordinary vendor invoices.

## Cares about

Seeing real progress and a named engineer. Gives up if they can't tell what they
paid for or what happens next.
