## Needs

- To buy engineering capacity and book it into a week straight away, so work
  starts without the wait of hiring.
- To see what's still needed before work can start, and what happens next.
- To know what each piece of the product will cost and how many weeks it takes,
  from Exalynt's estimates, so they can plan within tight money.
- To see every week who's on each block, what it's about, and when it's
  fulfilled, so they can tell what the money bought.

## Knows

Their market and what the product should do. Semi-technical: reads some code and
may have sketched a prototype with AI. New to Exalynt's terms.

## Context

Laptop and phone, once or twice a week around checkpoints, fitted between
customer and investor conversations. Money is tight and things feel urgent.

## Expects

To talk in MVPs and roadmaps, iterate quickly, and compare Exalynt with hiring a
contractor.

## Cares about

Visible progress every week for the money spent. Gives up if they can't tell
what they paid for or what happens next, or if starting work feels slow.
