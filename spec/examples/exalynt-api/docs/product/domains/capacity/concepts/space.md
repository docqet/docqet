A space has a name, which is its organization's when it's made, and the people
put in it, each with a [space membership](space-membership.md). It holds the
[conversations](conversation.md) about its organization's blocks.

A space stands on its own: an organization points at its space, but the space
doesn't belong to anything, so it outlasts everyone in it. Its people are the
organization's own and the engineers on the [teams](team.md) assigned to it, so
it's the one place a client and Exalynt's people share.

What's said in a space stays with the messaging provider. The app keeps only
which space is which, and who it put there.
