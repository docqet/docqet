description: >-
  What a [capacity block](capacity-block.md)'s work is about: a title, an
  optional description, and the images, videos, and files that go with it.
confused-with:
  - concept: concept:estimate
    summary: >-
      an estimate's focuses are how planned work is grouped and sized. A
      block's focus says what one block's work is about, and isn't taken
      from an estimate.
---

A focus belongs to the [organization](../../accounts/concepts/organization.md) whose blocks it's on, and
its images, videos, and files are that organization's uploaded files. Each image
or video can have a caption. It's its own record and can be on several of its
organization's blocks, such as work that runs over several weeks, while a block
has at most one focus. Taken off its blocks, a focus remains.
