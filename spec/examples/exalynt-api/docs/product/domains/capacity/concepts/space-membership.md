Each membership is one person in one space. Exalynt's people are put in
directly. A client's people may be invited instead, where the messaging provider
requires it, and are in once they accept. Someone the messaging provider can't
reach, such as a client with no account there, can't join and is emailed
instead.

## Lifecycle

1. Invited
2. Member

Accepting the messaging provider's invitation makes someone a member. Someone
who can't join stays unable to until they're added again. Being taken out of the
space ends a membership at any point.
