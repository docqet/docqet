id: last-in-first-out-with-an-exact-fit-first
name: Last in, first out, with an exact fit first
why: >-
  The clients who scheduled first keep their place, and as little room as
  possible is given up.
---

When blocks must come out of a week to fit less room, the latest-scheduled
block whose size exactly matches the shortfall comes out first, even if later
blocks are skipped. If none matches, the latest-scheduled block comes out, and
the choice repeats until the week fits.
