---
id: reductions-make-room-by-taking-blocks-out
name: Reductions make room by taking blocks out
why: >-
  A week is never overbooked, and clients keep their place in a week wherever
  a teammate can still fit them.
---

A reduction that leaves too little room for what's already booked takes blocks
out until it fits, and only once the engineer has seen which blocks those are
and confirmed. The engineer's own blocks that no longer fit their week come off
them first, staying in their week for a teammate to claim. Only if their team's
room is still short are blocks unscheduled, back to their clients to schedule
again.
