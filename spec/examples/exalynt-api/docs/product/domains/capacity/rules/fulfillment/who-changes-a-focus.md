id: who-changes-a-focus
name: Who changes a focus
---

Only someone who can manage a block it's on, or the block's client, changes a
focus. At the client, that's a member whose role lets them change focuses,
which the Admin and Member roles do, on any of its blocks, whether or not an
engineer is on it yet. Whoever can change the focus of any of a client's blocks
can look through that client's focuses to choose one. Anyone who can read the
block, the client included, reads its focus, and another organization can't.
