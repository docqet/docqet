---
name: Capacity payout basis
description: >-
  The amount that engineer, team-lead, and partnership shares of a block are
  calculated from.
confused-with:
  - concept: capacity-block@capacity
    text: what a client buys, at a price the basis doesn't depend on.
  - concept: payout-share
    text: one person's or partnership's part of the basis, not the basis itself.
---

A [capacity block](../../capacity/concepts/capacity-block.md)'s basis comes from its
[capacity units](../../capacity/concepts/capacity-unit.md), not from the price the client paid. Each
[payout share](payout-share.md), team support included, is a percentage of it.
