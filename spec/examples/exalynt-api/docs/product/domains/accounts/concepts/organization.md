---
name: Organization
description: >-
  An organization is a client of Exalynt: the account its people share, which
  buys capacity and is billed.
confused-with:
  - concept: team@capacity
    summary: >-
      an organization's members are its own people. A team is Exalynt's
      people, assigned to work with it.
  - concept: partnership@partnerships
    summary: >-
      a partnership is its own record, separate from any client
      organization.
---

It holds members, each with one organization [role](role.md), and pending
invitations for people who haven't joined yet. A person can belong to several
organizations, with a role in each. Engineering [teams](../../capacity/concepts/team.md) are assigned to
it, and a client can have any number of them. Its billing details are held by
the payment provider.
