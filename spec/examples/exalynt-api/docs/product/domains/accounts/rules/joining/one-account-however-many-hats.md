---
name: One account, however many hats
---

A person has one account, however many hats they wear. Someone who already has
an account and takes on another persona keeps that account and gains the apps
and functions the new one needs, alongside what they already have. An engineer
invited to be a partner gains the Partner Hub. A client member invited to be an
engineer gains the console. An engineer who signs up as a client creates an
organization from the account they already have.
