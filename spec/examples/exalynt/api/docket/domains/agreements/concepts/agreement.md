- **Agreement Template**: a type of agreement's text.
- **Agreement Version**: one version of a template.
- **Standard Terms**: what every party of a type shares.
- **Commercial Terms**: what's agreed with the named party, as a list of terms
  chosen from a catalog, such as an Engineer Share or a Referral Share. Each term
  applies only if it's listed.
- **Accepted Agreement**: the exact text and Commercial Terms someone accepted,
  kept as accepted.

A shared list of definitions gives the meaning of the words every agreement uses.
A [payout share](../../pay/concepts/payout-share.md) comes from the Commercial Terms of the agreement
in effect.

## Lifecycle

1. Offered
2. Accepted
3. In effect
4. Superseded

An agreement is offered to a party, who accepts it. The latest accepted is in
effect, until a later one is accepted and supersedes it.
