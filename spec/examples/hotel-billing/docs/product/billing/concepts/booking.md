# Booking

What a guest is charged for a stay: its nights, at the rate agreed when it was
booked.

## Details

A booking puts one room charge per night on the guest's [bill](bill.md), at its
agreed rate, whatever the rate is on the night.

## Not to be confused with

- **The booking app's booking:** a guest's hold on a room. Billing doesn't
  keep rooms free or taken, only what each stay costs.
