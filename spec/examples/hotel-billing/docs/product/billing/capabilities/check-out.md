# Check-out

Guests settle their bill as they leave, so nothing is left owing once they've
gone.

## What people can do

### Front desk clerk

- See a departing guest's bill, with every night and charge on it.
- Take payment for the balance, and close the bill.

## Learning

- Do guests want their bill emailed as they leave, or printed at the desk?
