# Split bills

Clerks split a bill between guests sharing a room, or move charges onto
another guest's bill.

## What people can do

### Front desk clerk

- Split a bill evenly between the guests in a room.
- Move a charge onto another guest's bill.

## Learning

- Do guests split evenly, or charge by charge?
