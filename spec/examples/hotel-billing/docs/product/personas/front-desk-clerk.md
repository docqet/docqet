## Knows

What every extra costs, and which guests' companies pay for their stays. Not an
accountant: they think in bills, not ledgers.

## Context

At the desk, with the guest in front of them and often a queue behind. Check-out
mornings are the busiest part of their day.

## Expects

A bill that reads like a receipt: each night, each extra, then the total.
Payments by card at the desk.

## Cares about

A bill the guest doesn't argue with. A charge they can't explain, or one they
can't take off when it was a mistake, makes for a hard conversation.
