# Check-out

Guests settle their bill as they leave, so nothing is left owing once they've
gone.

## Covers

- Clerks see a departing guest's bill, with every night and charge on it.
- Clerks take payment for the balance, and close the bill.

## Learning

- Do guests want their bill emailed as they leave, or printed at the desk?
