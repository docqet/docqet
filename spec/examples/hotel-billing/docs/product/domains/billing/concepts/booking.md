---
name: Booking
description: >-
  What a guest is charged for a stay: its nights, at the rate agreed when it
  was booked.
confused-with:
  - term: The booking app's booking
    text: >-
      a guest's hold on a room. Billing doesn't keep rooms free or taken,
      only what each stay costs.
---

A booking puts one room charge per night on the guest's [bill](bill.md), at its
agreed rate, whatever the rate is on the night.
